Living With Ahead

Built for the reality of 3 AM lows and sudden spikes.

Ahead was designed on the kitchen counter at 3 AM while drinking juice boxes. Here is how it feels and works in real daily life.

Kinetic Alerts

Quiet nudges before sirens fire.

When Ahead detects a steep rate of change (like -2.5 mg/dL/min), it doesn't wait for your blood sugar to reach 65 mg/dL. It calculates the decay slope and gives you a gentle vibration while you're still at 115 mg/dL.

That 25-minute head start means 8 grams of fast-acting carbs can gently level you out at 90 mg/dL without rebounding to 240 mg/dL from over-treating a terrifying crash.

Zero Blind Spots

Dual-sensor grace period handoff.

Dexcom G7 offers a 12-hour grace period when a sensor expires. Ahead allows you to insert and warm up your new sensor while your old sensor continues streaming readings to your screen.

No 30-minute blackout where you don't know your numbers, and no jittery initial warmup spikes catching you off guard.
