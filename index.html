What Ahead actually does

No filler features. Three things, built to be right.

Trend detection

Alerts that know the difference

A tiered system watches both where your glucose is projected to land and how fast it's moving — so a slow drift gets a nudge, and a real drop gets your phone's full attention.

Interactive reports

Built for the appointment, not just the app

Time in range, GMI, glycemic variability, and your full glucose trace, in one link your care team can open on any device — no export, no login wall.

Works with what you have

Runs alongside your CGM's own app

Ahead reads from Health Connect — it doesn't replace your sensor's built-in alarms or ask you to trust a new device. It's a companion, not a walled garden.

Fun Mode

Meet Pat.

Diabetes is serious enough. The app doesn't have to be. Fun Mode puts Pat — Ahead's glucose companion — front and center. He reacts to your numbers in real time: drinking juice during a low, giving himself insulin for a high, happy-dancing when you're in range.

Same data. Different energy. Because 2am lows are easier with a friend.
