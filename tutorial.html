⏳
The Rule of the 2nd Reading

On early Yellow alerts while in safe territory (120–140 mg/dL), pausing for Reading #2 gives the curve time to show if it is already braking naturally, preventing massive 250+ rebound spikes.

🚨
Urgent RED Demands Immediate Action

When glucose is already low or plunging towards ≤55 mg/dL, never wait. Treat immediately with 15g fast-acting carbohydrates.

🛑
Never Stack Rapid Insulin

Rapid insulin takes 60–90 minutes to peak. Stacking another bolus 45 minutes after a meal turns a temporary spike into a severe hypoglycemia crash 2 hours later.
