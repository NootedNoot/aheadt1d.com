Ecosystem Comparison

Ahead vs. Ahead Lite: What Each Is & How They Differ

We built two specialized apps to solve two distinct needs in diabetes care. Here is how they compare so you know exactly which to install.

Primary Device Engine Standalone

Ahead (The Autonomous Monitor)

Ahead is a self-sufficient, heavy-duty continuous monitoring system built to live on the primary phone carried by the person with Type 1 Diabetes. It is designed to work even in the middle of nowhere without cellular reception.

  • ✓
    Direct Sensor Pairing: Reads directly from Dexcom G7/G6 or FreeStyle Libre 2/3 via Google Health Connect or Bluetooth LE. Does not depend on the internet.
  • ✓
    Dual 15m & 30m Projections: Continuously computes forward velocity and acceleration, calculating both 15-minute and extended 30-minute horizons.
  • ✓
    7 Distinguishable Acoustic Tones: Custom harmonic waveforms (descending tones for lows, ascending for highs, rapid pulses for urgent drops) so you can tell what is happening by sound alone.
  • ✓
    Custom Tripwires & DND Override: Set custom numeric thresholds or rate-of-fall triggers (-2.0 mg/dL/min) that break through Do Not Disturb.
  • ✓
    24/7 Watchdog Service: An Android foreground service with automatic wake-up watchdog ensures Android never kills background monitoring.
Best For: The individual living with T1D. Install Ahead on your main phone that goes with you everywhere.
Caregiver & Glanceable Companion Cloud Synced

Ahead Lite (The Glanceable Companion)

Ahead Lite is an ultra-lightweight, zero-friction visual dashboard and follower app. It is designed for caregivers, secondary phones, desk displays, or nightstand tablets that need glanceable glucose without battery drain.

  • ✓
    Cloud Follower Architecture: Streams verified glucose points from Ahead Cloud API (ahead-backend) or Nightscout so loved ones stay informed remotely.
  • ✓
    Zero-Impact Battery Footprint: Eliminates continuous Bluetooth radio polling and heavy CPU wakelocks, conserving battery on older or budget devices.
  • ✓
    Instant High-Contrast Dashboard: Large crisp glucose readout, current trend slope (+1.6 mg/dL/min), 15m projection, and a clean historical sparkline curve.
  • ✓
    Injected Test Data Safeguards: Communicates with Ahead via on-device IPC. If synthetic test scenarios are injected, Ahead Lite shows a clear warning banner (🚨 INJECTED TEST DATA ACTIVE) to prevent follower confusion.
  • ✓
    Zero-Friction Pairing: No sensor calibrations, Health Connect permissions, or Bluetooth pairing required—just connect to your cloud stream.
Best For: Parents, spouses, caregivers, secondary desk monitors, or anyone who wants an uncluttered glucose dashboard.

Setup Guide

How to Install on Android

Ahead is distributed as a direct standalone APK. Follow these 4 quick steps to install and set up your sensor feed.

1

Download the APK

Tap the download button above directly from your phone's browser (Chrome, Samsung Internet, or Firefox) to save ahead-latest.apk.

2

Allow Unknown Apps

Open the downloaded file. When prompted by Android security, tap Settings and toggle "Allow from this source" to complete installation.

3

Connect Health Connect

Open Ahead and follow the quick setup wizard to grant Blood Glucose read access in Google Health Connect (synced from Dexcom or Juggluco).

4

Exempt from Battery Saver

When prompted, set Ahead to Unrestricted battery optimization so Android doesn't kill the 24/7 background monitor overnight.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about sideloading, privacy, and safety boundaries.

Why is Ahead distributed as an independent APK?

Distributing directly as an open APK allows rapid safety updates, exact-alarm watchdogs, and direct BLE integration without waiting weeks for restrictive app store review queues.

Does Ahead replace my official CGM receiver or insulin pump?

No. Ahead is an independent monitoring companion engineered for early awareness, trend extrapolation, and personalized acoustic feedback. It does not replace your CGM manufacturer's native urgent alarms or the clinical guidance of your endocrinologist.

How does Ahead protect my health privacy?

Ahead operates on a strict local-first architecture. All reading archives, step correlation metrics, and trend math are stored in your device's private SQLite vault. No health telemetry or personal data is ever sold or tracked.

How do I update to newer builds?

When an update is released, simply download the new APK from this page and tap install. Android will seamlessly update Ahead in-place without touching your preferences, history, or custom thresholds.

Can I install both Ahead and Ahead Lite on the same device?

Yes. Ahead (com.aheadt1d.app) and Ahead Lite (com.aheadt1d.lite) have separate package IDs and install side-by-side with complete isolation. When run on the same phone, Ahead Lite uses local ContentProvider IPC to safely coordinate test scenarios and display safety disclaimers whenever synthetic data is active.

Which app should parents, partners, or caregivers install?

Caregivers should install Ahead Lite. It streams glucose points remotely from Ahead Cloud or Nightscout with near-zero battery drain, instant glanceable typography, and automatic alerts without requiring local sensor calibration or Bluetooth management.

Medical Disclaimer: Ahead is a monitoring companion and research tool designed for informational purposes. It does not replace your CGM's native alarms, your insulin delivery system, or the guidance of your physician.
