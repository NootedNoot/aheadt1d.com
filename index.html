The Real Problem

Why traditional CGM alarms fail when you need them most

Standard CGM apps treat your body like a static snapshot. They compare a single discrete reading against fixed thresholds (like 70 or 180 mg/dL). If you are falling at -3.5 mg/dL every minute, waiting for 70 means you will already be in an incapacitating low by the time your carbs digest.

Standard Alert Apps

Reactive Alarm Model

Alerts only trigger after 70 mg/dL is breached. No consideration of acceleration, recent insulin boluses, or rapid drops.
