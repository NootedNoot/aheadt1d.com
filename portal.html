Getting Started: Connect Ahead to your Phone

Download APKs

You now have an Ahead account! Follow these 3 simple steps to start streaming real-time glucose and generating your 14-day clinical reports:

1 Install the Apps

Download Ahead and AheadBLE on your Android phone from the Downloads page.

2 Sign In on Ahead

Open Ahead on your phone and log in with your email and password. Your phone links automatically.

3 Connect CGM / Sensor

Pair AheadBLE to your Dexcom G7 sensor or grant Health Connect. Your glucose will appear here instantly!
